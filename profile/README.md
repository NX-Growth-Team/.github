## What we do

Growth is rarely one big idea. It is a steady loop of asking a clear question, instrumenting the answer, and shipping the change that follows. We work across three layers of that loop:

| Layer | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Experimentation** | Hypothesis design, funnel instrumentation, A/B tests, and readouts that end in a decision |
| **Product analytics** | Metric definitions, event models, cohort analysis, and dashboards people actually use |
| **Systems & automation** | Internal tools, referral and lifecycle pipelines, and AI-assisted workflows that remove manual work |

## How we work

- **Metric first.** Every initiative starts with the number it is meant to move.
- **Small and reversible.** We favor narrow changes we can ship, measure, and undo.
- **Measure, then decide.** A test that settles the question is a good test, whatever the result.
- **Documented by default.** Specs, decisions, and readouts live next to the work they describe.
- **Privacy is a design constraint.** Customer data stays where it belongs, and access is explicit rather than assumed.

## Projects

Most of our repositories are private while they are under active development. Our work falls into three groups:

- **Growth platforms** — referral and lifecycle systems with segmentation, campaign tooling, and reconciliation.
- **AI-assisted operations** — internal agents and workflows that sit directly next to real business data.
- **Product surfaces** — UX and funnel improvements backed by instrumentation rather than opinion.

If you would like a walkthrough of any of these, get in touch.
